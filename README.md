Continuous monitoring of soil conditions and surrounding environmental parameters enables data-driven land stewardship, carbon sequestration verification, and early detection of ecological degradation. This list is intended for environmental scientists, pedometricians, agronomists, IoT engineers, and ecological researchers.

## Soil Analytics & Pedometrics

Libraries, data pipelines, and analytical frameworks for mapping soil chemistry, physical properties, and spatial variability.

- [aqp (Algorithms for Quantitative Pedology)](https://github.com/ncss-tech/aqp) — R package collection designed to model, visualize, and analyze soil profile data and pedon attributes.
- [soilDB](https://github.com/ncss-tech/soilDB) — Open-source R package for fetching soil survey data directly from USDA-NRCS, SoilWeb, and global databases.
- [SoilDataR](https://github.com/pedometrics/soilDataR) — Data harmonization engine for compiling, standardizing, and processing multi-source soil property datasets.
- [ISRIC SoilGrids API Engine](https://github.com/ISRICWorldSoil/SoilGrids250m) — Spatial processing framework for extracting high-resolution global soil map predictions.

## Sensor Hardware & IoT Networks

Hardware schematics, firmware modules, and sensor interfaces for in-situ soil property monitoring and environmental logging.

- [Open-Sensors-Environmental](https://github.com/enviro-diy) — Open-source hardware platform for building low-cost, modular environmental dataloggers and sensor arrays.
- [Modular Sensor Library](https://github.com/42Hz/ModularSensors) — Arduino-compatible library for managing environmental sensors, telemetry modules, and low-power logging routines.
- [Cosmic-Ray Neutron Sensing Tools](https://github.com/CRNS-Tools) — Processing algorithms for cosmic-ray neutron sensors measuring field-scale soil moisture content non-invasively.

## Environmental Data Platforms

Open data portals, spatial viewing engines, and cloud repositories dedicated to environmental baseline data.

- [OpenGEO Environmental Platform](https://github.com/opengeos) — Geospatial analysis tools for querying and processing climate data, land surface temperature, and canopy coverage.
- [Global Soil Information System (GloSIS)](https://github.com/FAO-GSP) — Data exchange standards and spatial infrastructure developed under the FAO Global Soil Partnership.

## Modeling & Carbon Accounting

Computational models for forecasting soil organic carbon (SOC) dynamics, greenhouse gas flux, and microclimate dynamics.

### Soil Carbon & Organic Matter

- [RothC Implementation](https://github.com/RothC-Model) — Python and R implementations of the Rothamsted Carbon Model for turnover of organic matter in non-waterlogged soils.
- [DNDC (DeNitrification-DeComposition)](https://github.com/dndc-model) — Process-based model simulating carbon and nitrogen biogeochemistry in terrestrial ecosystems.

### Microclimate & Atmospheric Modeling

- [Microclimate Modeling Toolbox](https://github.com/NicheMapR/NicheMapR) — R package for calculating microclimate temperature and moisture profiles experienced by organisms at the ground surface.
